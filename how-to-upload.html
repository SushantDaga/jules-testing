How to Upload GPX Files

Once you've generated your GPX file with FakeMyRun, you can upload it to popular running apps like Strava and Runkeeper. Here’s how:

Uploading to Strava

  1. Log in to your Strava account on the Strava website (uploading GPX files is not currently supported on the mobile app).
  2. In the top right corner, hover over the plus icon and select "Upload activity."
  3. [Strava upload menu image placeholder]
  4. Click on "File" on the left sidebar.
  5. Click "Choose Files" and select the GPX file you downloaded from FakeMyRun.
  6. [Strava file selection image placeholder]
  7. Review the activity details, make any necessary edits (e.g., activity name, type, description), and click "Save & View."

For more detailed instructions, you can visit Strava's official support page: Strava Support - Uploading a File

Uploading to Runkeeper

  1. Log in to your Runkeeper account on the Runkeeper website.
  2. Click on the "Log" tab in the top navigation menu, then select "Activities."
  3. Click the "+ Activity" button, which is typically found near the top right of your activity feed or calendar.
  4. In the activity creation pop-up, look for an "Upload Map" or "Import from GPX" option.
  5. [Runkeeper upload option image placeholder]
  6. Click "Upload File" or a similar button, then choose the GPX file from your computer.
  7. Once uploaded, you can edit the activity details (e.g., title, notes, type of activity).
  8. Click "Save" to add the activity to your Runkeeper log.

For more information, check out Runkeeper's support resources: Runkeeper Support
