How FakeMyRun Works

The Run Generator

Create Custom Running Routes

With FakeMyRun, you have the power to design your ideal running experience. Our intuitive map interface allows you to plot out any route you can imagine, whether it's a scenic trail through the mountains, a challenging city loop, or a leisurely jog by the beach. Simply click to add points, adjust the path, and see your creation come to life in real-time.

Generate Realistic GPX Files

Once you've perfected your route, FakeMyRun generates a standard GPX (GPS Exchange Format) file. This file contains all the crucial data points – latitude, longitude, elevation, and timestamps – that running apps use to track and display your activities. Our sophisticated algorithms ensure that the generated GPX files are virtually indistinguishable from those recorded by actual GPS devices, providing a seamless experience with your favorite fitness platforms.

How the Technology Works

Our route generation technology uses advanced pathfinding algorithms and elevation data to create realistic running profiles. When you design a route, we interpolate points to ensure smooth curves and natural-feeling changes in pace and elevation. The GPX file is then constructed according to industry standards, making it compatible with a wide range of running apps and devices.

The Token System

Pay-Per-Download with Tokens

FakeMyRun operates on a flexible token system. Instead of a recurring subscription, you purchase tokens that can be redeemed to download your generated GPX files. Each route download costs a certain number of tokens, giving you control over your spending. This pay-per-download model ensures you only pay for what you actually use.

Token Storage

Your purchased tokens are securely stored in your FakeMyRun account. You can view your token balance at any time and purchase more tokens as needed. We utilize industry-standard security practices to protect your account and token information.

Email Synchronization

Link Tokens to Your Email

To make managing your tokens even easier, you can link them directly to your email address. This means your token balance is associated with your email, providing a convenient way to access them across different devices or if you ever need to recover your account.

How Email Synchronization Works

When you create an account or purchase tokens, you'll have the option to associate them with your email. If you choose to do so, our system securely links your token balance to your provided email address. This allows for easy sign-in and ensures that your tokens are always accessible when you log in with that email.
